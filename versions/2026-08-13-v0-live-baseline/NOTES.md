## What it is

The site as it stood before any portfolio work: a name, a photo, and four nav links —
*who i am* · *what i'm doing (like a bvlogythingy)* · *resume* · *linkedin*.

The resume link pointed **offsite** to a published Google Doc:
`docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vRKU8nVZhI7LAXVV97DTH1zuAhpVU77nSba5c30LrHQBgZEDu46W2gXeQQLq8gSnuNF6vwIrRZIVeqM/pub`

Zero projects shown. No contact mechanism. No statement of what Brendan does.

## Audience

**Undefined — and that was the finding.** A peer portfolio review (Arya, CCSF, Aug 2026)
asked who the site was trying to attract and the reviewer circled *"Other: Not really sure."*
On whether it achieved a portfolio that attracts its audience: *"Maybe."*

Reviewer's comment, verbatim: the austere nature made them wonder whether it would attract
anyone to interview him.

## What the review asked for

- It reads as aimed at technical jobs, not production roles
- Add a contact box — fewer clicks than routing through the resume
- A better photo in the about section
- More projects on the home page

## Known problems at snapshot time

1. The linked Google Doc was **stale** — it predated the Aug 2026 resume rebuild, so the
   site was publishing a resume with errors that had already been fixed elsewhere.
2. No project cards at all, on a site functioning as a portfolio.
3. No through-line: nothing told a visitor what Brendan does or what to do next.
